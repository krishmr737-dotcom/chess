Know the format

League rules

🔁

Double round robin

Every player faces each opponent twice. For N players, each player has 2 × (N − 1) games.

🏆

Points system

Win = 2 points · Draw = 1 point · Loss = 0 points.

🤝

Fair play

Agree on match deadlines and time control. No outside assistance during games.
